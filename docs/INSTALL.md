# Installing Abode

Get the latest version from the **[download page](https://github.com/asmorris/abode/releases/latest)**.
Scroll to **Assets** and pick the file for your computer.

Abode isn't from the App Store or Microsoft Store, so the first launch needs
one extra click. After that it opens normally, and it updates itself.

## Mac

1. Download **`Abode-…-mac.dmg`** (works on both newer Apple-chip Macs and older Intel ones).
2. Open it and drag **Abode** onto the **Applications** folder.
3. Open Abode from Applications (or Launchpad).
4. The first time, macOS says it can't check Abode for malicious software.
   Click **Done** (not "Move to Bin"), then:
   - open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**,
   - scroll down to *"Abode" was blocked…* and click **Open Anyway**,
   - enter your password and click **Open**.

   On older macOS (before 15), you can instead right-click Abode in
   Applications, choose **Open**, then **Open** again.

You only need to do this once.

## Windows

1. Download **`Abode-…-windows-setup.exe`**.
2. Open it. If Windows shows *"Windows protected your PC"*, click
   **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. Click **Install**. Abode is added to the Start menu and your desktop.

No administrator password is needed; Abode installs just for you.

## Updates

When there's a new version, Abode shows **Update** on its start screen and in
the sidebar. Click it: Abode downloads the new version, installs it and
restarts. Your houses aren't touched.

You can also check by hand: **⋯ menu → Check for updates…**.

## Where your houses are

Abode saves houses in **Documents → Abode** by default. Each house is a single
`.abode` file you can copy, back up or email to someone else who has Abode.

## Uninstalling

- **Mac**: drag Abode from Applications to the Bin.
- **Windows**: Settings → Apps → Abode → Uninstall.

Your house files in Documents → Abode are kept either way.
